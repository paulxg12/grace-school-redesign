Volunteer of Grace (VOG)

Volunteer of Grace is a long-lasting relationship platform for all who have studied, taught, learnt, loved or have contributed to Grace School. It's a family that stays connected forever.

Whether you were a student, teacher, staff member, donor, or well-wisher — VOG is your home. Stay connected with old friends, contribute to the growth of the school, and continue to be part of the Grace School legacy.
