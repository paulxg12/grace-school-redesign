Our Story

Welcome to Grace School

Grace School, in Gopalganj, Bihar, India, was conceived and founded in the year 1998. It was registered in 2002 and is recognized by the Department of Education, Government of Bihar, India. The school is managed by SFORCE (Social Forum for Reformation, Charity and Education).

Now in its 26th year of service, Grace School is a Christian-based elementary school dedicated to providing quality education through continuous innovation. We believe in nurturing young minds with faith, hope, and love — building strong foundations for life.

Our institution is committed to academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth of every child entrusted to our care.

Admissions

The Admission & Education Process

Transparent, inclusive, and child-centered — our approach to education.

Admissions at Grace School operate on a first come, first served policy, ensuring fairness and equal opportunity for all families seeking quality education for their children.

We comply fully with the Right to Education (RTE) Act 2009, reserving seats for economically weaker sections and disadvantaged groups as mandated by law.

Our curriculum follows the NCERT framework, delivered by an innovative teaching team that blends traditional values with modern pedagogical methods. Our teachers are trained to inspire curiosity, critical thinking, and a love for learning.

From early childhood education to upper primary classes, every stage of learning is carefully designed to meet the developmental needs of each child.

Governance

Accountability, Administration & Management

Transparent governance and dedicated leadership drive our institution forward.

7
Executive Committee Members

Our Executive Committee comprises seven dedicated individuals who provide strategic direction, governance oversight, and policy guidance to ensure the school operates with integrity and purpose.

Permanent Admin Team Members

A core team of five permanent administrators manages day-to-day operations, ensuring smooth functioning across academic, financial, and administrative domains with accountability and transparency.

Our Accountability

Grace School maintains the highest standards of accountability through regular audits, transparent financial reporting, parent-teacher meetings, and community engagement. We believe in being answerable to the families and communities we serve, ensuring that every resource is utilized effectively for the benefit of our students.
