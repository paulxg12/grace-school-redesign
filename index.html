About Grace School

Building Strong Foundations
for Life…since 1998

We believe in learning, listening, sharing and caring. We aspire to love, respect, understand and encourage. We are here to gently inspire.

Grace School, in Gopalganj, Bihar, India, was conceived and founded in the year 1998. It was registered in 2002 and is recognized by the Department of Education, Government of Bihar, India.
