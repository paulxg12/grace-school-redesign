“We believe in learning, listening, sharing and caring.”

Grace School was conceived and founded in Gopalganj, Bihar, in 1998 — born from a vision to bring quality, value-based education to a region where it was needed most.

Registered in 2002 and fully recognized by the Department of Education, Government of Bihar, Grace School is managed by SFORCE (Society for Reformation, Conservation & Education). As we step into our 26th year, we remain rooted in faith and rising with purpose.

We are proud to be one of Bihar's most trusted institutions, committed to nurturing every child's mind, body, and spirit.

NCERT RTE Govt. Recognized Est. 1998

Foundation

Faith, hope, love, care, academics, activities, health and sports — the bedrock of every Grace student's journey.

Purpose

Self-discovery, career exposure, goal setting, smart and independent skills — preparing students for life beyond school.

Our Legacy

Planted with Love, 1998.

Grace School was founded by visionary hearts who believed that every child deserves a nurturing education rooted in faith. Ciara Pearman, Angela Kelly, and Anupam Yunas planted the seeds of what would become a lasting legacy of love, learning, and community in Gopalganj, Bihar.

Their unwavering dedication gave rise to an institution that has shaped thousands of lives over two and a half decades — a school where children don't just learn, they flourish.
